FROM COMH

About

Comh is a collective of contemporary artists formed while studying together at the National College of Art & Design (NCAD), Dublin. Comh take their name from the Irish for “together” or “shared”. The collective combine a shared ideology of collaboration and a studio environment that fosters dialogue, critique, and exchange, while maintaining individual authorship of their work.

FROM COMH brings together a culmination of a year’s work, showcasing how each artist interprets and explores a wide range of themes from memory and identity, landscape and portraiture, to history and legend, interwoven with elements of water and land. The work ranges across media, from installation and film work, to paintings, sculptures, drawings, prints and book-making. FROM COMH brings together these parallel practices, exploring how proximity and shared context can shape distinct artistic outcomes.

Comh believe that art is for everyone. For more details of the accessibility programme, artists’ talks, education outreach series, and more, see comh.ie or email comhcollective@gmail.com.
